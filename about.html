Hi, I’m Aria.

I’m from China and I’m studying Digital Media at the University of Leeds. My background is in media, and I’m interested in creative ways to tell stories.

I enjoy indie games, console games and tabletop role-playing games. I also like drawing, photography, films and animation.

I’m learning how to turn ideas into small digital projects. This portfolio is a space to share that process, from early sketches to finished work.

What I’m exploring

  • Visual storytelling
